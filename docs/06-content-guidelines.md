# Vietnamese Content Guidelines

## Tone

- Professional
- Clear
- Trustworthy
- Practical
- Local-business friendly
- Avoid exaggerated claims

## CTA examples

Use factual, direct CTAs such as:
- Gọi ngay để được tư vấn
- Liên hệ nhận báo giá
- Yêu cầu tư vấn
- Liên hệ thuê xe

## Pricing

Never publish rental prices unless the product requirements explicitly change.

## Claims

Do not invent:
- years of experience
- number of vehicles
- customer counts
- certifications
- safety records
- partner/customer logos
- geographic coverage

Only use verified company-provided information.

## SEO

Write naturally for Vietnamese search intent. Avoid keyword stuffing and mass-generated repetitive pages.
